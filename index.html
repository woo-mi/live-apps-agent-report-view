Recommended action

01
Prioritize admin reactivation before explorer reactivation.
Portfolio ratios are comparable, but 7 of 20 admin-active accounts sit below typical against 3 of 23 explorer-active. The immediate weakness is admin consistency by account, not overall volume.
02
Start with Inhabit for a role-wide intervention.
It is below typical for both roles — 10 of 27 admins and 4 of 12 explorers active in 7 days — making it the single clearest cross-role adoption risk in the portfolio.
03
Treat Conoco and Blend as explorer expansion, not dormant accounts.
Conoco has the second-largest explorer base at 174 monthly actives but only 44.25% recency; Blend has 98 monthly explorers at 46.94%. Lift here improves both scale and recency.
04
Use Nykaa, Propertyfinder, Rubrik and Cisco as success patterns.
Nykaa and Propertyfinder pair large explorer bases with strong ratios, Rubrik holds the largest admin base at 65.91%, and Cisco is strong on explorers while typical on admins.

Method and caveats

Covers the last 7 and 30 days of qualifying activity; an active user has at least one qualifying event in the period. Accounts restricted to instances.type = customer, with internal users, instances and partners excluded, and low-value infrastructure events filtered out.

Roles resolve from userRoleIds; a dual-role event counts in both segments. Engagement ratio is active_users_7d ÷ active_users_30d, banded Below-typical <0.40, Typical 0.40–0.55, Strong 0.55–0.70, Exceptional >0.70.
